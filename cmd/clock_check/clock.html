The delay is the big clock's number minus the number visible inside each video. Example: 42.50 − 40.20 = 2.30 seconds behind.

Warnings appear after 1.5 seconds without a new presented picture, while this page can run. A hidden tab, suspended browser, paused player or unavailable observation is labelled separately. Recovery needs one second of advancing pictures. A still scene is normal: new frames can show the same objects.

Wait for both videos to settle. A screenshot containing all three clocks gives an approximate manual measurement. Use it to check the automatic test. Playback warnings alone cannot detect a frozen camera that keeps sending new timestamps; the pattern test can measure the growing age while its pattern remains readable.

Automatic measurement results and limits

The typical value is the median of successful samples' midpoints. The largest upper value is the largest sampled marker interval endpoint, not a maximum for the system. Failed reads are excluded from those summaries and counted separately. A hidden page, interrupted browser or reconnected viewer ends the test. Starting another test clears these results. The check reads at most twice per second per picture, so it can miss short events.
