Compare with the GStreamer picture

  1. Place the GStreamer video window beside this page. Keep the white clock visible and the page in the foreground.
  2. Wait for the picture to settle. Take one screenshot that includes this clock and the clock inside the video.
  3. Subtract the number inside the video from the number on this page.

Example: 42.50 − 42.20 = 0.30 seconds behind (300 milliseconds).

This is an approximate check. Screen refresh, camera exposure, video frame timing and screenshot timing add uncertainty. Repeat it several times; one screenshot cannot prove a maximum delay.

This page only displays a clock. It does not play video, calculate delay automatically or change camera settings. Keep it visible during a check; if the browser or computer pauses, start a new check after it resumes.
